### Simplified Project Summary

The project aims to create an **AI-assisted creative attire design system** that helps designers create **culturally appropriate, body-personalized, visually realistic, and affordable costumes and garments**.

The system focuses on **cultural costumes, festival attire, national costumes, pageant outfits, and heritage-inspired clothing**.

The main problem with existing AI tools is that although they can generate attractive clothing designs, they may:

- **Get cultural details wrong** — mixing traditions, patterns, colors, or symbols from different cultures.
- **Create culturally inappropriate designs** — especially when dealing with sacred or sensitive cultural elements.
- **Ignore the actual human body** — producing garments that look good digitally but may not fit or be wearable.
- **Ignore production costs** — generating elaborate designs without considering fabric, materials, labor, or budget.
- **Provide little explanation** — designers cannot easily understand why the AI selected a particular cultural element, material, or design.

### What the Proposed System Does

The proposed system combines **human expertise and AI** rather than replacing the designer.

It would use several technologies:

1. **Cultural Heritage Knowledge Graph (CHKG)**
   Stores structured and authoritative information about cultures, traditions, textiles, motifs, colors, symbols, and appropriate uses.

2. **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**
   Helps connect the AI with reliable cultural and design knowledge instead of relying only on general AI knowledge.

3. **Generative AI**
   Creates clothing concepts based on the designer's requirements and verified cultural information.

4. **Computer Vision / Body Analysis**
   Analyzes a person's body proportions to help create more personalized and realistic designs.

5. **Predictive Costing Engine**
   Estimates fabric, materials, labor, and overall production costs.

6. **Explainable Design Evaluation**
   Explains why certain cultural elements, materials, colors, or design features were selected and evaluates the resulting design.

### Overall Workflow

The system essentially follows this process:

**Cultural Research → Body Analysis → Design Requirements → AI Design Generation → Cost Estimation → Cultural & Design Evaluation → Virtual Visualization → Human Designer Review → Refinement**

### In One Sentence

> **The project proposes a human-AI collaborative system that helps designers create culturally responsible, body-fitted, visually realistic, explainable, and cost-conscious creative attire by combining cultural knowledge graphs, generative AI, computer vision, predictive costing, and human expertise.**

### Even Simpler Version

Think of it as a **“smart AI fashion design assistant”** specifically designed for cultural and creative attire.

Instead of simply telling an AI:

> _“Create a Filipino-inspired pageant costume.”_

the system would first check **which cultural elements are appropriate**, consider the **wearer's body**, consider the **available budget and materials**, generate the design, and then **explain and evaluate the design** before the human designer makes the final decision.
